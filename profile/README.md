# VIB-CCB BioIT

Bioinformatics tools, pipelines, benchmarks and AI skills from the VIB Center for Cancer Biology.

## Tools & pipelines
Browse everything developed in the center — public repositories and in-house pipelines you can ask about:

**[Catalog of tools & pipelines](https://github.com/VIB-CCB-BioIT/tools-catalog)**

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Single-cell transcriptomics | [Bartab](https://github.com/VIB-CCB-BioIT/tools-catalog#single-cell-transcriptomics), Pan-cancer downstream analysis |
| Spatial omics | Spatial transcriptomics, metabolomics, multiplex imaging |
| Long-read & DNA sequencing | Lrsomatic, ASCAT.sc, nanoWGS |

| Imaging & microscopy | napari-fast4dreg, nd-registration |
